    Frequently asked questions

    Minimum company size

    3 employees. Sole proprietors and partnerships with 3 or more staff qualify.

    What the call covers

    Headcount, current cover and renewal date, and which benefits to include. The call takes 20 minutes. Quotes from 3 insurers follow within 5 working days.

    Foreign employees

    Employment Pass, S Pass and Work Permit holders can be covered on the same plan.

    How employees get the Insider Pass

    Each covered employee receives an invite on enrolment. Every code and link sits on their member page.

    Joiners and leavers

    Add or remove employees each month. Premiums adjust pro rata. The pass stays active until the end of the month an employee leaves.

    Cost of the Insider Pass

    Included on every group plan at no extra cost. Individuals can join for S$240 a year.

    About Benefixe

    Group insurance and perks for Singapore companies. Compared across insurers, run from one page, reviewed before every renewal.

    What we stand for

    A benefits plan is a promise to every employee. We keep it plain enough to read in one sitting, priced against the market, and useful between claims.

    • Independent

      Independent. Every quote compares 3 insurers on the same specification. You see the full table, including the options we did not recommend.

    • Plain

      Plain. Each plan arrives as one page: what is covered, what is not, and the cost per employee.

    • Used

      Used. The Insider Pass gives every employee 5 perks they reach for weekly, so the plan is felt before anyone files a claim.

    • Accountable

      Accountable. Claims and perk usage are reviewed 60 days before renewal, with a written recommendation.

    Partnership with your company

    Insurer panel. We place cover with established Singapore insurers and quote the same specification across them, so price and terms compare like for like.

    Claims support. Employees submit through one page. We follow up with the insurer until each claim is settled.

    Perk partners. Insider Pass partners are added through the year at no extra cost to the company.

      Covers

      Every cover your team needs. One renewal.

      A Benefixe plan brings hospital and surgical, outpatient GP and specialist, term life, personal accident and dental cover into one policy year. Every covered employee also receives the Insider Pass, with 5 perks.

      Hospital.

      Room and board to the ward class you choose, surgery, ICU, and treatment before and after a stay.

      Outpatient.

      GP and specialist visits, tests and prescribed medicine, for everyday care.

      Life and accident.

      A lump sum to the family, and cover for accidental injury at work or outside it, 24 hours a day.

      Dental.

      Check-ups, fillings and extractions at panel clinics.

      Take a closer look.

      Review. We read the policy schedule, 12 months of claims and the staff list, and mark cover that overlaps or falls short.

      Compare. Three insurers quote one specification. The table shows premium per employee, limits and exclusions side by side.

      Enrol. Employees receive cover cards and Insider Pass invites on the first day of the policy.

      Savings

      Pay for the cover your team uses.

      Most of the savings in a review come from cover bought twice across separate policies, ward classes and limits that do not match how the team claims, and renewals accepted without a comparison. A review checks all three before you sign.
