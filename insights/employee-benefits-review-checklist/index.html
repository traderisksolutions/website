Employee benefits review: seven things to check before renewal

By the TRS broking team24 Oct 20265 min read
The short answerStart a benefits review at least 90 days before renewal. Check headcount and data accuracy, claims paid against premium, plan features employees actually use, gaps for senior staff and dependants, and whether the insurer’s service has worked when it was needed.

Why start early?

Insurers need accurate census data and claims history to quote. Starting 90 days out leaves time to approach several insurers and adjust the plan.

The checklist

Work through these before asking for quotes.

#Check
1Census data: headcount, ages, joiners and leavers
2Claims paid compared with premium
3Benefits used most, and least
4Gaps for senior staff and dependants
5Panel clinics and how employees claim
6Group personal accident and travel cover
7Service: how quickly claims and changes were handled

What changes most often at review?

Outpatient design, co-payments and ward entitlement are the usual levers. Changes should be explained to employees before the renewal date.

Frequently asked questions

Can benefits change mid-year?

Additions and removals of employees happen through the year. Plan design normally changes at renewal.
