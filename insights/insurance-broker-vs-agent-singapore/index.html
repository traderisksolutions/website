Insurance broker vs agent in Singapore: who acts for you and how each is paid

By the TRS broking team24 Oct 20265 min read
The short answerAn agent represents one or more insurers and sells their products. A broker represents the client, compares cover across insurers and must be registered with MAS. Brokers are usually paid commission by the insurer, sometimes a fee from the client, and disclose how they are paid.

Who does each represent?

An agent acts for the insurer. A broker acts for the business buying insurance, which matters most when terms are negotiated and when a claim is disputed.

When does a broker add the most?

When cover is complex, several insurers need comparing, or a claim needs arguing on the wording. Liability, construction, marine, cyber and employee benefits are typical examples.

How to check a broker?

Confirm registration on the MAS register, ask which insurers they place with, and ask how they are paid.

Frequently asked questions

Does using a broker cost more?

Commission is usually already built into the premium. Ask the broker to disclose how it is paid.
