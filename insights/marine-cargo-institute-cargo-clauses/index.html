Marine cargo insurance: Institute Cargo Clauses A, B and C compared

By the TRS broking team24 Oct 20265 min read
The short answerInstitute Cargo Clauses A cover all risks of loss or damage to goods in transit, subject to listed exclusions. Clauses B and C cover only named events, with C the narrowest. The Incoterm in the sale contract decides whether the buyer or seller should insure.

How do A, B and C differ?

All three exclude things like poor packing, delay and inherent vice.

ClausesScope
ICC AAll risks, subject to exclusions
ICC BNamed perils, including fire, sinking, collision, earthquake, water entry
ICC CFewer named perils; no cover for water damage or theft

Who should insure: buyer or seller?

Under CIF and CIP, the seller arranges insurance for the buyer. Under FOB and similar terms, the buyer carries the risk once goods are loaded and should arrange cover.

Single shipment or open cover?

Regular shippers usually take an open policy that covers every shipment automatically within agreed limits, instead of insuring each one.

Frequently asked questions

Does cargo insurance cover delay?

No. Loss caused by delay is excluded under all three sets of clauses.
