Contractors’ all risks insurance for fit-outs and construction projects

By the TRS broking team24 Oct 20265 min read
The short answerContractors’ all risks covers physical loss or damage to the works during construction, and usually third-party liability arising from the project. It runs from the start of work to handover, often with a maintenance period after. Main contracts usually say who must arrange it.

What does CAR cover?

Section 1 covers the works, materials on site and, if added, plant and equipment. Section 2 covers liability to third parties for injury or property damage caused by the project.

Who arranges it?

The contract decides: sometimes the owner, more often the main contractor, naming subcontractors and the owner as insureds.

What is commonly excluded?

Defective design and workmanship itself, wear and tear, and loss found only at stock-taking. Some policies cover damage resulting from defects; check the defects clause.

Frequently asked questions

Does CAR cover workers’ injuries?

No. Employee injuries fall under WICA and employer’s liability insurance.
