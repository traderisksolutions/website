# Business
Corp Cover is a Singapore commercial insurance brokerage for SMEs.
Operated by Trade Risk Solutions Pte Ltd, a registered insurance broker.
Quotes from 5 or more insurers on every placement.
Response within 1 working day for enquiries under S$50,000 premium.
No fee to the client; the broker is paid by the insurer.

# Work injury compensation (WICA)
Mandatory for every employee doing manual work, and for non-manual employees earning S$2,600 a month or less.
Minimum cover S$45,000 per employee for medical expenses.
Penalty for non-compliance: fine up to S$10,000, jail up to 12 months, or both.
Typical premium for a 10-person office: from S$450 a year. [pricing note, may be quoted]

# Public liability
Covers third-party injury and property damage arising from business operations.
Landlords in Singapore commonly require S$1m to S$5m per occurrence in the lease.
Excess usually S$500 to S$2,000 per claim.

# Professional indemnity
Claims-made policy: the policy in force when the claim is made responds, not the one in force when the work was done.
Retroactive date matters on a switch of insurer.

# Directors and officers
Protects directors' personal assets against claims from shareholders, regulators and employees.

# Property and business interruption
Fire, extended perils, all risks. Business interruption indemnity period 12 months is the common default.
