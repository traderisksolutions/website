# Business
Benefixe arranges group employee benefits for Singapore companies from 5 to 500 staff.
Operated by Trade Risk Solutions Pte Ltd, a registered insurance broker.
Every plan is quoted at 5 insurers: AIA, Income, QBE, Raffles Health Insurance, Singlife.
One membership with 18 perks is included free on every Benefixe group benefits plan: eSIM data, bike rental, gym classes and more.
Quote returned within 2 working days from a staff list with dates of birth.

# Group hospital and surgical (GHS)
Pays hospital and surgical bills for employees, and dependants where added.
Plans are set by ward: private 1-bed, private 4-bed, government A, government B1.
Co-payment plans cost less than full-cover plans. [unverified: the saving varies by insurer; confirm before quoting a percentage]
Pre-existing conditions: most insurers cover after 12 months of continuous membership.

# Group term life (GTL) and critical illness (GCI)
Lump sum on death or diagnosis, usually 12 to 36 times monthly salary or a flat S$50,000 to S$300,000.
GTL and GCI premiums are GST-exempt in Singapore.

# Outpatient GP, specialist and dental
Outpatient GP from S$6 to S$20 a visit at panel clinics; specialist by referral; dental caps of S$200 to S$500 a year.

# Medical cover for Work Permit and S Pass holders
Employers must hold medical insurance of at least S$60,000 a year per Work Permit and S Pass holder, from 1 July 2023.
GHS-FW plans meet the Ministry of Manpower requirement.
