The sector

How financial services businesses buy insurance

Financial firms in Singapore buy insurance because MAS, a fund's investors or a counterparty requires it. A licensed financial adviser with revenue of S$5 million or less must hold professional indemnity of S$1 million. A retail fund manager must hold S$2 million, rising to S$25 million with assets under management. Registered insurance brokers must hold professional indemnity under the Insurance (Intermediaries) Regulations. Fund side letters and institutional due diligence questionnaires ask for D&O and crime limits before an allocation is made.

The programme is not one policy. Professional indemnity answers client claims for negligent advice or breach of mandate. Directors and officers cover answers regulatory inquiries and shareholder actions against individuals. Crime cover pays the firm's own loss from employee dishonesty or forged instructions. Cyber pays breach response and PDPA costs. All are claims-made: the policy in force when the claim is made responds, for acts after the retroactive date. The gaps are usually a deductible above the MAS cap, a fund entity missing from the D&O, or an uninsured outsourced administrator.

Named risks

The exposure, what it costs, the cover that answers it

Negligent advice or breach of mandate
A client alleges the portfolio was run outside the mandate or the advice was unsuitable. Defence costs start at the first letter and the claim is measured against the client's loss.
Professional indemnity at or above the MAS minimum, with defence costs in addition to the limit and the deductible under 20 per cent of base capital.
Regulatory investigation
MAS begins an inspection that leads to a formal inquiry. Directors incur legal costs before any finding, and the company may be unable to indemnify them.
D&O with Side A cover for individuals and a regulatory investigation costs extension that triggers at the first formal request.
Employee or third-party fraud
A staff member diverts client money, or a forged payment instruction is acted on. The loss is the firm's own and no liability policy responds.
Commercial crime cover for employee dishonesty, forgery, funds transfer fraud and social engineering, with a limit sized to the largest single payment.
Client data breach
KYC files and account data are exfiltrated. The PDPC must be notified within 3 calendar days once the breach is assessed as notifiable, and the penalty cap is 10 per cent of Singapore turnover.
Cyber liability with breach response costs, regulatory defence and a limit sized to the number of client records held.
Licence condition shortfall
An audit finds the professional indemnity limit or deductible below the MAS requirement. The firm is in breach of a licence condition until it is corrected.
Professional indemnity placed to the MAS scale for the licence type, reviewed at every change in assets under management or revenue.
Loss of a key portfolio manager
The founder or lead manager dies or is incapacitated. Investors have a key person clause and can redeem or suspend the fund.
Keyman insurance on the named individuals at a sum that funds the transition period the fund documents allow.

Questions

Questions financial services buyers ask

What professional indemnity limit does MAS require for a licensed financial adviser?

For advisers with revenue of S$5 million or less, S$1 million. Above S$5 million, the lower of 20 per cent of gross revenue or S$10 million. A firm that only issues research needs S$500,000. Base capital is S$500,000, or S$300,000 with an additional S$500,000 of professional indemnity insurance.

What are the MAS professional indemnity rules for fund managers?

MAS may impose a licence condition requiring a retail licensed fund management company to hold professional indemnity on a scale from S$2 million below S$100 million of assets under management to S$25 million at S$10 billion and above, with the deductible no more than 20 per cent of base capital. Managers serving accredited and institutional investors are strongly encouraged to hold it and must disclose their arrangements, or the absence of cover, to every customer.

What does claims-made mean and why does the retroactive date matter?

A claims-made policy responds to claims first made during the policy period, not when the work was done. The retroactive date is the earliest date of work the policy will cover. If it is set at the current renewal rather than the firm's founding date, advice given in earlier years is uninsured. Continuous cover without a gap keeps the original date.

Does D&O cover the fund as well as the management company?

Only if the fund entities are named as insureds. A management company D&O covers its own directors. Fund directors, general partners and investment committee members need the fund vehicles scheduled on the policy, or a separate fund D&O. Investment management insurance packages do this in one wording.

What must we do after a data breach under the PDPA?

Assess whether the breach is notifiable within 30 calendar days of discovering it. If it causes or is likely to cause significant harm, or affects 500 or more individuals, notify the PDPC within 3 calendar days of that assessment and notify affected individuals as soon as practicable. Penalties for contraventions from 1 October 2022 reach 10 per cent of annual Singapore turnover where turnover exceeds S$10 million, or S$1 million otherwise.

Sources: MAS, financial adviser's licence requirements, MAS, Guidelines on Licensing and Conduct of Business for Fund Management Companies (SFA 04-G05), MAS, insurance broker registration, Personal Data Protection Act 2012
