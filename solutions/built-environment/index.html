The sector

How built environment businesses buy insurance

Contractors, developers and consultants in Singapore buy insurance because the contract says so. The Public Sector Standard Conditions of Contract 2020 require the contractor to insure the works and third-party liability for the contract period. Tenders ask for a performance bond and an insurance schedule at award. MOM requires work injury compensation for every site worker, plus a S$60,000 medical policy and a S$5,000 security bond for each non-Malaysian Work Permit holder. Main contractors push the same requirements down to every sub-contractor.

The programme is not a single site policy. Contractors' all risks (CAR) covers the works and materials for physical damage; public liability covers third-party injury and damage, including neighbouring buildings; a performance bond secures the employer against default; work injury compensation and Work Permit cover handle the workforce; professional indemnity answers design claims. CAR is bought per project on the contract value or annually on turnover. The gaps are usually vibration and weakening of support, a maintenance period that ends before defects liability, and bond capacity exhausted before the next tender.

Named risks

The exposure, what it costs, the cover that answers it

Damage to the works
A collapse, flood or fire destroys completed sections and materials on site. The contractor must reinstate at its own cost before the employer pays again.
Contractors' all risks at full contract value with escalation, plus testing, commissioning and a maintenance period matching the defects liability period.
Damage to neighbouring property
Piling or excavation cracks an adjacent building or sinks a road. Claims arrive from owners, the authority and the utility.
Public liability with vibration, removal or weakening of support included, at a limit at least equal to the contract requirement.
Worker injury or death
A worker falls or is struck on site. For accidents from 1 November 2025, WICA death compensation reaches S$269,000, and the WSH Act fine for a company reaches S$500,000 on a first conviction.
Work injury compensation from an MOM-designated insurer for every worker, plus the Work Permit medical policy and security bond per worker.
Bond call
The employer calls the performance bond after a delay or dispute. An on-demand bond pays first and argues later, and a bank bond ties up the facility.
Surety bond from an insurer with conditional or on-demand wording as the contract permits, keeping the bank line free for working capital.
Design error
A consultant or design-and-build contractor specifies a member that fails or a system that cannot be certified. Rectification and delay costs follow.
Professional indemnity on a claims-made basis with a retroactive date that covers every live project.
Plant and equipment
A hired crane or an owned excavator is damaged or stolen on site. The hire contract makes the contractor liable for the full value.
Contractors' plant and equipment cover on owned units and a hired-in plant section at the hirer's replacement value.

Questions

Questions built environment buyers ask

Does CAR cover the defects liability period?

Only if a maintenance period of the same length is written into the policy. The construction period ends at practical completion. A maintenance clause then covers damage the contractor causes while rectifying defects, and an extended maintenance clause also covers damage arising from a cause during the construction period. The policy period must run to the end of defects liability, including any extension of time.

Is an insurance bond or a bank guarantee better for a performance bond?

An insurance surety bond does not reduce the bank facility or require cash collateral, so working capital stays free. A bank guarantee is usually on demand and secured against the facility. The contract decides what is acceptable: PSSCOC and most private contracts accept a bond from an approved insurer. The surety underwrites the contractor's financials and track record before issuing.

Who buys WICA cover for sub-contractors' workers?

Each employer must hold its own work injury compensation policy. MOM treats the cover as employer-centric, so a main contractor's policy does not cover a sub-contractor's employees unless the policy is extended to name them. The main contractor should collect a current WICA certificate from every sub-contractor before mobilisation, because an injured worker can claim against the principal if the sub-contractor is uninsured.

What does the S$5,000 security bond do?

It is MOM's security for each non-Malaysian Work Permit holder. It is forfeited if the employer or worker breaks the permit conditions, salary is paid late, the worker goes missing, or the worker is not repatriated when the permit ends. The employer cannot recover the cost from the worker. An insurer issues the bond for a premium and the employer remains liable to reimburse any forfeiture.

Can one annual policy cover all our projects?

Yes. An annual CAR policy covers every contract started in the year up to a stated contract value and period, declared on turnover. It suits contractors with many small and medium jobs. A project exceeding the policy limits is placed separately. Public liability and work injury compensation are already annual, so the annual CAR removes the per-project paperwork at award.
