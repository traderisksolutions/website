The sector

How energy and chemicals businesses buy insurance

Energy and chemical operators in Singapore insure because lenders, landlords and MOM require it. A plant that stores or processes dangerous substances above the threshold quantities is a major hazard installation under the Workplace Safety and Health (MHI) Regulations and must keep a safety case. Leases and project financing agreements set minimum property and liability limits. Contractors on site do manual work, so work injury compensation insurance is compulsory for each of them. A single shutdown can cost more in lost margin than in repair.

The programme is not a general business package. It is a property damage policy on the plant, a business interruption section with an indemnity period (the months of lost profit the insurer pays), machinery breakdown for turbines, compressors and transformers, and pollution liability for sudden and gradual release. Property is bought at declared reinstatement value with a deductible per event. The gaps are usually values not updated after a capacity upgrade, an indemnity period shorter than the lead time for replacement equipment, and contractors' cover never checked against the site's limits.

Named risks

The exposure, what it costs, the cover that answers it

Fire or explosion at the plant
A release ignites and damages process units, tankage and neighbouring property. Repair takes months, and the WSH Act fine for a corporate body reaches S$500,000 on a first conviction.
Property damage on an all-risks basis at full reinstatement value, with a per-event deductible the balance sheet can carry.
Prolonged shutdown
A damaged compressor has a nine-month lead time and the plant runs at half capacity. Fixed costs and contracted supply obligations continue without the margin.
Business interruption with an indemnity period at least as long as the longest replacement lead time, plus an increased cost of working sub-limit.
Machinery breakdown
A turbine, transformer or reactor fails from an internal cause with no fire. Standard property policies exclude mechanical and electrical breakdown.
Machinery breakdown cover scheduled by unit, with business interruption following breakdown added as an extension.
Pollution release
A spill or vapour release reaches a neighbour, a waterway or the sea. The agencies order clean-up and third parties claim for damage.
Environmental impairment liability for sudden and gradual pollution, because public liability wordings limit pollution to sudden and accidental events if they cover it at all.
Contractor injury on site
Turnaround and construction crews are covered by WICA through their own employer, and the site owner is drawn into every serious incident. Permanent incapacity compensation now reaches S$346,000.
Work injury compensation for own staff, with a contract term that every contractor produces a current WICA certificate before mobilising.
Construction and commissioning
A new unit, solar array or battery system is damaged during erection, testing or commissioning. The operating property policy does not attach until handover.
Contractors' all risks or erection all risks for the project period, with testing and commissioning and a maintenance period included.

Questions

Questions energy and chemicals buyers ask

Is pollution covered under our public liability policy?

Usually only for sudden and accidental events, and many wordings exclude pollution entirely. Gradual seepage, long-term contamination and agency-ordered clean-up need environmental impairment liability. It is written on a claims-made basis, meaning the policy in force when the claim is made responds, so a retroactive date before the site started operating matters.

How long should the business interruption indemnity period be?

At least as long as the time to source, ship, install and recommission the critical unit with the longest lead time, plus the time to rebuild the market. Insurers ask for the figure in months. A period chosen to save premium that ends before the plant is back at full output leaves the balance of the loss with the company.

What makes a site a major hazard installation?

MOM classifies petroleum refining, petrochemical manufacturing and chemical processing plants, and any installation storing or using toxic or flammable substances at or above the threshold quantities, as MHIs. MHIs must keep and maintain a safety case under the Workplace Safety and Health (MHI) Regulations. Insurers underwriting an MHI ask for the safety case and the loss prevention record.

Do we need separate cover for a solar or battery project?

Yes. During construction the project needs contractors' all risks or erection all risks, including testing and commissioning. After handover it moves onto the operating property policy, with machinery breakdown for inverters and transformers and business interruption for lost generation revenue. The two policies must meet at the handover date without a gap.

Can insurance pay a WSH Act fine?

No. Fines are not insurable. Under the WSH Act a corporate body faces a fine of up to S$500,000 on a first conviction and S$1 million as a repeat offender; an individual faces up to S$200,000 and two years' jail. Liability policies can pay legal defence costs for the company and D&O can pay them for directors and managers.
