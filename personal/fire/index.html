The cover

Fire insurance in Singapore

Fire insurance pays to reinstate the structure of a home after fire and the other perils listed in the policy, such as lightning, explosion and burst pipes. Two rules force the purchase in Singapore. HDB flat owners with an HDB loan that started on or after 1 September 1994 must hold the HDB Fire Insurance Scheme policy, underwritten by Etiqa since 16 August 2024, for as long as the loan runs. Banks require a mortgagee interest policy on every private property and bank-financed flat they lend against.

Fire insurance is not home contents insurance. It excludes renovations, furniture, appliances and personal belongings, and the HDB scheme also excludes alternative accommodation and liability to neighbours. The sum insured is the cost to rebuild the structure, not the market value; a bank may set it at the outstanding loan instead. The HDB policy runs 5 years and most bank policies 1 year. TRS checks the sum insured against reinstatement cost, names the bank as mortgagee and adds the non-cancellation clause the lender asks for.

Who buys it

  • HDB flat owners with an HDB loan that started on or after 1 September 1994.
  • Private property owners whose bank requires a mortgagee interest policy before drawdown.
  • HDB owners on a bank loan, where the bank sets its own fire cover condition.
  • Owners who have repaid the loan and want structure cover to continue.
  • Landlords insuring the structure of a leased unit separately from the tenant's contents.

What the insurer needs to quote

  • HDB scheme account number, or postal code and unit number
  • Bank letter of offer stating the fire insurance condition
  • Latest valuation report or reinstatement cost estimate
  • Property type, floor area and year of completion
  • Existing fire or mortgagee policy and its expiry date
  • Name of the mortgagee bank to be endorsed on the policy

Price and terms

What moves the premium

Flat type
HDB scheme premiums are fixed by flat type, from S$1.11 to S$6.68 for 5 years including GST.
Reinstatement cost
Bank policies price on the cost to rebuild; a higher valuation or a revaluation raises the sum insured and the premium.
Property type
Strata apartments are insured at the lower of reinstatement value or outstanding loan; landed homes at full reinstatement value excluding land.
Perils added
Extending the base fire cover to burst pipes, flood or impact widens the policy and moves the rate.
Who arranges it
A bank-arranged policy is priced by the bank's panel insurer; an owner-arranged policy must still name the bank as mortgagee and may carry a bank administration fee.

At renewal

Three mistakes we see most often

Letting the HDB policy lapse
The HDB scheme does not renew itself and cannot be paid from CPF. Diarise the 5-year expiry and pay the cash premium online or at an AXS kiosk before it ends.
Insuring at loan balance only
A sum insured set at the outstanding loan protects the bank, not the owner, and leaves the rebuild short once the loan has been paid down. Insure at full reinstatement cost and review it after each revaluation.
Treating fire cover as home insurance
Neither the HDB policy nor a bank mortgagee policy pays for renovations, contents or a hotel while the flat is repaired. Add a home contents policy that covers renovation works at their invoice cost.

Questions

Questions buyers ask

Is HDB fire insurance compulsory?

Yes, if the flat has an HDB housing loan that started on or after 1 September 1994. The policy must be bought and renewed every 5 years until the loan is repaid. Owners on a bank loan or with no loan are not required to hold it, but may buy it. The appointed insurer since 16 August 2024 is Etiqa. The premium is paid in cash, not from the CPF Ordinary Account.

What does the HDB fire insurance policy cover?

It pays to reinstate the internal structure, fixtures and fittings originally provided by HDB or its approved developers after fire and the listed extraneous perils, including fire spreading from an adjoining unit and water from burst pipes. Sums insured run from S$37,900 for a 1-room flat to S$176,700 for an Executive flat. It does not cover renovations, furniture, personal belongings, alternative accommodation or liability to neighbours.

What does a bank mean by a mortgagee interest policy?

A fire policy on the mortgaged property with the bank named as mortgagee. If damage stops the borrower servicing the loan, the bank claims against the insurer and the borrower stays liable for the balance. Banks set the sum insured at reinstatement cost, or for strata apartments at the lower of reinstatement cost and the outstanding loan. The policy must carry a clause requiring the insurer to notify the bank before cancellation.

Can I buy fire insurance from my own insurer instead of the bank's panel?

Usually yes, with the bank's approval. The policy must name the bank as mortgagee, include a non-cancellation clause and reach the bank before the current cover expires; Standard Chartered asks for the documents 5 business days ahead and charges a S$100 administration fee a year. If the owner's policy lapses, the bank arranges cover itself. HDB loan flats have no choice: the scheme policy must come from Etiqa.

Does the MCST fire policy on my condominium cover my unit?

The MCST policy insures the building and common property on behalf of all owners. The bank still requires a mortgagee interest policy on the unit, because the two policies protect different interests: the strata body on one side, the lender's charge over the unit on the other. Renovations and contents inside the unit fall under neither and need a home contents policy.

What happens if I do not renew the HDB fire insurance?

The flat is uninsured for structural damage and the condition attached to the HDB loan is no longer met. HDB sends a renewal notice before expiry, but renewal is not automatic. Reinstate the policy through Etiqa online, the AXS app or an AXS kiosk, using the HDB scheme account number or the postal code and unit number. The new policy runs 5 years from its own start date.
