Coverage

What fire insurance covers

Building Structure

Financial protection to rebuild or repair the physical walls, floors, and ceilings of your property following an insured event.

Original Fixtures & Fittings

Coverage for built-in wardrobes, flooring, and kitchen counters originally provided by the developer or HDB.

Debris Removal Expenses

Covers the cost of clearing structural debris and site clean-up required before any reinstatement or reconstruction can begin.

Alternative Living Costs

Immediate financial support for emergency living expenses if a fire renders your primary residence structurally unsafe.
