What maid insurance covers

MOM Security Bond & Letter

Direct issuance of the mandatory $5,000 letter of guarantee to the Ministry of Manpower on your behalf.

Hospital & Surgical Coverage

High-limit inpatient medical care, day surgery, and pre/post-hospitalization expense coverage to safeguard your helper's health.

Wages & Levy Reimbursement

Financial compensation for your helper's salary and the monthly government levy during periods where she is hospitalized.

Employer Liability Protection

Indemnifies your household against costly legal claims or common law lawsuits arising from workplace injuries or accidents.
