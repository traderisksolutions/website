TRS is an insurance brokerage for businesses, based in Singapore. For over 15 years TRS has placed corporate programmes for companies from early-stage technology start-ups to institutional enterprises.

TRS acts for the client, not the insurer. The team works as an extension of the client's finance or operations function: it structures the cover, negotiates the premium and argues the claim.

TRS is independent. There are no volume quotas and no carrier alignments. Each placement is compared across 18 Singapore-licensed insurers, including AXA, Chubb and Allianz, and decided on the wording and the price.

TRS writes in plain terms. Before anything is signed, the policy is summarised into what is covered, what is excluded, the deductibles and the limits.

TRS is fast without cutting corners. Specialists work on digital workflows, so comparative quotes from several insurers and a compliant policy binder arrive within 48 hours.

TRS stays for the long term. As headcount, sites and operations change, the programme is audited twice a year so the cover keeps pace with the business.
