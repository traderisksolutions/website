What public and products liability insurance covers

Public & Premises Liability

Protects your business if a visitor, client, or vendor suffers an accidental injury on your corporate premises or project sites.

Products & Completed Operations

Indemnifies your firm against legal claims alleging bodily injury or property damage caused by a product you manufactured, sold, or distributed.

Defective Workmanship Liability

Coverage for legal liabilities arising from property damage caused by your operational services or completed contracts.

Worldwide Jurisdiction Defense

Covers the immense legal fees, court costs, and court-ordered settlements associated with defending major corporate lawsuits.
