The cover

Foreign worker security bond in Singapore

A foreign worker security bond is the S$5,000 guarantee MOM requires from an employer for each non-Malaysian Work Permit holder. It is a promise to pay the Government if the employer or the worker breaks the Work Permit or bond conditions. An insurer's guarantee replaces a bank guarantee or cash deposit, so the employer keeps its cash. The trigger is the In-Principle Approval: the bond must be lodged with MOM and in force on the day the worker lands, or immigration will not admit the worker.

It is not insurance for the employer's own loss: if MOM forfeits the bond, the insurer pays MOM and recovers the sum from the employer under a counter-indemnity. The bond runs for a fixed term matched to the permit, is bought per worker, and is discharged about one week after the permit is cancelled and the worker has left Singapore, provided no condition was breached. The broker lodges the bond electronically, tracks expiry against the permit, and handles the discharge or any forfeiture notice.

Who buys it

  • Any employer bringing in a non-Malaysian Work Permit holder, before the worker arrives
  • Construction, marine shipyard and process contractors onboarding crews in bulk
  • Manufacturing and services firms renewing Work Permits on a rolling basis
  • Employers who prefer not to lock S$5,000 per worker in a bank guarantee
  • Households employing a migrant domestic worker from outside Malaysia

What the insurer needs to quote

  • In-Principle Approval letter or MOM renewal notice for each worker
  • Company UEN, SSIC code and CPF submission number
  • Worker name, FIN and nationality
  • Required start date, at least three working days ahead
  • Any past bond forfeitures or MOM enforcement action

At renewal

Three mistakes we see most often

Lodging too late
The bank or insurer needs up to three working days to send the bond details to MOM, and the bond must be in effect when the worker arrives. Buy the bond as soon as the In-Principle Approval is issued, not when the flight is booked.
Treating the bond as cover for the employer
Forfeiture is a payment to MOM, and the insurer claims the full sum back from the employer under the counter-indemnity. Keep salary on time, repatriate on expiry and report a missing worker at once; those are the conditions that protect the bond.
Forgetting discharge
A bond stays live until the permit is cancelled and the worker has left; an expired bond on a renewed permit breaks a Work Permit condition. Diary the permit renewal and the bond expiry together, and ask for the discharge letter after departure.

Questions

Questions buyers ask

How much is the bond and who pays it?

S$5,000 for each non-Malaysian Work Permit holder, bought from any bank or insurance company as a banker's or insurer's guarantee. The employer pays; MOM states you cannot ask the worker to pay for it. Malaysian Work Permit holders and S Pass holders do not need a bond.

When must the bond be in force?

Before the worker arrives in Singapore. After purchase, the bank or insurer sends the details to MOM, which can take up to three working days, and the bond must take effect on the day of arrival. If it is not in effect, the immigration officer will not allow the worker to enter.

What causes the bond to be forfeited?

MOM may forfeit the bond if the employer or worker breaks a Work Permit or bond condition, the salary is not paid on time, the worker is not sent home after the permit expires, is revoked or is cancelled, the worker goes missing, or a construction, marine shipyard or process worker is not sent for the Onboard programme.

Am I liable if the worker breaks the rules?

Not for the worker's own violations, if you can prove you informed the worker of the Work Permit conditions and reported the violation to MOM when you first became aware of it. Keep a signed acknowledgement of the conditions on file and report a missing worker in writing the same day.

When is the bond released?

Only when the Work Permit has been cancelled, the worker has returned home and no bond condition was breached. MOM usually discharges the bond one week after the worker leaves Singapore and notifies the employer by post. The insurer then closes the bond; premiums already paid are generally not refunded.

Does the bond replace medical insurance or WICA?

No. The bond, the S$60,000 foreign worker medical insurance and work injury compensation insurance are three separate obligations. The medical policy details must be entered in WP Online before the permit is issued, and WICA cover applies to every manual worker regardless of nationality.
