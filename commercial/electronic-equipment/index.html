The cover

Electronic equipment insurance in Singapore

Electronic equipment insurance covers sudden physical damage to computers, servers, network hardware, medical imaging units, laboratory analysers, building control systems and broadcast equipment. It is an engineering line written on an all-risks basis rather than named perils, so short circuit, voltage surge, operator error, water leakage and impact are inside cover. In Singapore the buyers are clinics, laboratories, data-dependent offices and schools, often after a lease or an equipment lessor asks for proof of cover on hardware.

It is not cyber insurance or a maintenance contract: Singapore wordings carry an absolute cyber loss exclusion, and the cost of fixing functional failures or carrying out maintenance is excluded. The policy has three sections: material damage to the hardware, restoration of external data media, and increased cost of working to hire substitute equipment. Cover is annual at new replacement value per item, with a deductible per occurrence. The broker sets the three sums insured and confirms whether theft is added.

Who buys it

  • Clinics, dental practices and imaging centres with scanners, X-ray units and analysers.
  • Laboratories and test houses with calibrated instruments.
  • Offices, law firms and agencies whose servers and workstations run the business.
  • Schools and training centres with large fleets of computers and AV equipment.
  • Data centres, telecoms rooms and broadcast studios.
  • Companies leasing equipment under a contract that requires insurance in the lessor's name.

What the insurer needs to quote

  • Equipment list: make, model, year, serial number, replacement value
  • Location of each item and the room conditions
  • Maintenance contracts and backup procedures
  • Daily cost of substitute equipment and the indemnity period wanted
  • Value needed to replace data media and reproduce data
  • Claims in the last three years

Price and terms

What moves the premium

New replacement value
Premium is a rate on the total replacement value of the listed items, including freight, duties and installation.
Equipment class
Medical and laboratory systems, broadcast gear and portable equipment rate higher than fixed office hardware.
Environment
Air-conditioned rooms, surge protection, UPS and raised floors reduce the rate; a basement or ground-floor room raises it.
Theft and portable items
Adding theft and cover away from the premises for laptops and field instruments adds premium.
Data media and increased cost of working
The sums insured chosen for restoring data and hiring substitute equipment are rated separately.
Deductible and time excess
A higher material damage deductible and a longer time excess on increased cost of working lower the rate.

At renewal

Three mistakes we see most often

Assuming theft is covered
The standard electronic equipment wording in Singapore excludes loss or damage caused by theft; a stolen server is not a claim. Add theft by endorsement, or insure portable items under a separate all risks policy.
Data media without a backup routine
Section 2 pays to replace media and reproduce data only for costs incurred within 12 months, and excludes loss from false programming or inadvertent deletion. Set the sum insured at the real cost of reconstruction and keep an off-site backup, or the section pays for blank media only.
Increased cost of working set too low
The sum insured for Section 3 must equal 12 months of substitute equipment at the agreed daily rate; a low figure is scaled back in proportion when the hire runs long. Price the daily rental of a like-for-like system before setting the sum.

Questions

Questions buyers ask

What is the difference between electronic equipment and machinery breakdown insurance?

Machinery breakdown covers internal failures of mechanical and electrical plant and excludes external perils. Electronic equipment insurance is all risks: it covers external causes such as water, impact, fire and short circuit as well as internal failure, for low-voltage electronic systems. The two are not interchangeable; a chiller belongs on breakdown, a CT scanner on electronic equipment.

Does it cover power surges and voltage fluctuation?

Damage from short circuit, over-voltage or arcing inside the premises is covered as a sudden physical cause. Loss from the failure or interruption of the public electricity, gas or water supply is excluded in the standard wording, so an outage that corrupts a process without physical damage is not a claim. Install surge protection and a UPS; insurers ask about both.

How is a destroyed item valued?

The insurer pays the actual value immediately before the loss, which is the new replacement value less depreciation, plus dismantling, freight and installation where they are in the sum insured. Repairable damage is paid at repair cost with no depreciation on replaced parts. A full replacement value endorsement, where the insurer offers it, removes the depreciation deduction; ask for it on equipment under three years old.

What does the data media section pay?

The cost of replacing damaged external data media with new material and reproducing the lost information, after material damage covered under Section 1, limited to expenses incurred within 12 months of the event. If the data is not reproduced in that time, only the blank media is paid. Costs from false programming, inadvertent deletion or magnetic fields are excluded. Cover applies while the media is at the premises.

What is increased cost of working under this policy?

The extra spent to use substitute equipment after covered damage interrupts the insured system: hire of replacement servers, time on another facility's scanner, outsourced processing. It pays an agreed sum per day up to the annual sum insured, after a time excess, and only from the day the substitute is in use. Personnel and transport costs are added where separate sums are listed.

Is equipment on lease or hire covered?

Rented or hired equipment is excluded where the owner is responsible for it by law or under the lease or maintenance agreement. Where the lease puts the risk on the user, the item is listed with the lessor noted as loss payee. Check the lease: most require the lessee to insure at full replacement value and to name the lessor.
