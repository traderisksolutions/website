What cyber insurance covers

Ransomware & Extortion Response

Immediate access to specialized cyber-incident response teams, forensic investigators, and legal advisors to resolve ransom demands.

Replaces lost operational net profit and covers extra expenses if a cyberattack or system failure takes your digital networks offline.

Pays for legal defense, class-action settlements, and statutory penalties resulting from corporate data privacy breaches (e.g., PDPA).

Reimburses the technical costs required to safely decontaminate, rebuild, and restore your corrupted corporate databases and software systems.
