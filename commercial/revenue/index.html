What business interruption insurance covers

Gross Profit Protection

Indemnifies your business for the loss of net profit and turnover resulting from an insured physical disruption.

Standing Charges & Fixed Costs

Continuous funding to cover essential ongoing expenses, including payroll, rent, taxes, and bank loan obligations.

Increased Cost of Working

Coverage for emergency expenses incurred to minimize downtime, such as renting temporary offices or outsourcing production.

Interdependent Supply Chain Cover

Extended protection covering revenue losses caused by disruptions at a critical customer's or supplier's facility.
