The cover

Contractors' all risks insurance in Singapore

Contractors all risks (CAR) insurance covers physical damage to the contract works during construction and the contractor's legal liability to third parties on and around the site. In Singapore the purchase is forced by the contract. The Public Sector Standard Conditions of Contract (PSSCOC), used on government projects, obliges the contractor to insure the works in the joint names of employer and contractor before work starts, and private forms, landlords' fit-out guides and main contractors copy the same demand.

It is not work injury compensation, professional indemnity or motor cover, and it excludes the cost of fixing defective design, material or workmanship, though damage those defects cause to sound work is inside cover. The policy runs for the contract period plus the defects liability period, on a sum insured equal to the full contract value and a third-party limit per occurrence, with a deductible per claim. The broker matches the wording to the contract clause, names the parties and sets up any annual open cover.

Who buys it

  • Main contractors tendering for BCA, HDB, LTA or JTC projects under PSSCOC.
  • Interior fit-out firms working in malls, offices and shophouses under a landlord's fit-out guide.
  • M&E, piling, facade and specialist subcontractors required to carry their own cover.
  • Developers and owners arranging a project policy that names every contractor.
  • Renovation contractors on the HDB directory taking private residential jobs.
  • Firms with many small jobs a year that need an annual open cover with declarations.

What the insurer needs to quote

  • Letter of award or tender with the insurance clause
  • Contract sum, scope of works and bill of quantities
  • Site address, soil conditions and neighbouring structures
  • Programme: start, completion and defects liability period
  • Method statement for piling, demolition or hot work
  • Names of principal, consultants and subcontractors to be insured

Price and terms

What moves the premium

Contract value
The rate is applied to the full contract sum, including materials the principal supplies, plus professional fees and debris removal items.
Type of work
Piling, basements, tunnelling, demolition and work next to existing buildings rate higher than fit-out and above-ground structures.
Period and maintenance
A longer construction period and extended maintenance cover for the defects liability period cost more than maintenance visits cover only.
Third-party limit
Public projects commonly require S$10 million any one occurrence; a higher limit or cover for vibration and removal of support adds premium.
Deductibles
Higher deductibles for defective design, wet perils and underground services lower the rate.
Existing property
Cover for the employer's existing building on a first-loss limit, standard for fit-out and A&A work, is rated on that limit.

At renewal

Three mistakes we see most often

Policy that stops at practical completion
PSSCOC clause 28.1 requires the works to stay insured until 14 days after substantial completion and through the defects liability period for causes arising before it. A policy that ends on handover leaves the contractor in breach; buy the extended maintenance cover to match.
Employer not a named insured
The contract asks for joint names, cross liability and the employer noted as principal; the certificate names the contractor only. The employer can reject the policy and insure at the contractor's cost under clauses 27.3 and 28.1(3). Give the broker the clause and check the schedule against it.
Fit-out contractor relying on the landlord
A tenant's fit-out contractor assumes the building owner's property policy responds to damage it causes; it does not, and the landlord's insurer can recover from the contractor. Fit-out work needs its own CAR with existing property cover and the landlord named.

Questions

Questions buyers ask

What does PSSCOC require a contractor to insure?

Clause 28.1 requires the Permanent Works, Temporary Works and unfixed materials on site to be insured in the joint names of employer and contractor for not less than the Contract Sum plus the Appendix percentage for professional fees, from before work starts until 14 days after substantial completion and through the defects liability period. Clause 27 adds Work Injury Compensation Act cover and third-party liability with the employer as additional insured and cross liability.

Is the maintenance or defects liability period covered?

Only by extension. Maintenance visits cover pays for damage the contractor causes while carrying out defects work. Extended maintenance cover also pays for damage during the defects liability period from a cause on site before the completion certificate was issued. PSSCOC requires the second form, so contractors on public projects should ask for extended maintenance for the defects liability period stated in the Appendix.

Are defective design and workmanship covered?

The cost of replacing or repairing the defective item itself is excluded. Damage the defect causes to correctly executed parts of the works is covered, usually with a higher deductible; one public sector clause sets it at S$15,000 or 10% of the loss, whichever is higher. Claims against the designer for faulty design belong to professional indemnity insurance.

Does CAR cover my excavators, cranes and site plant?

Not automatically. PSSCOC clause 28.1 excludes tools and construction equipment from the works insurance, and the CAR wording excludes mechanical or electrical breakdown of plant and vehicles licensed for road use. Plant is insured as a separate item under Section I at new replacement value, or on a contractors plant and machinery policy that follows the plant from site to site.

What third-party liability does the policy give?

Section II pays damages for accidental bodily injury or property damage to third parties arising from the works, on or next to the site, plus defence costs. Damage from vibration, removal or weakening of support is excluded unless endorsed, and damage to underground cables and pipes is covered only where the contractor checked the utility plans first. Public contracts commonly set the limit at S$10 million any one occurrence.

Do I need CAR for a shop or office fit-out?

Landlords' fit-out requirements and public sector fit-out contracts ask for it; one university tender applies a S$2,000 deductible per occurrence to fit-out and renovation contracts. The policy should name the landlord as principal, cover the landlord's existing property on a first-loss limit and run until handover. Works that touch fire protection, escape routes or compartments also need SCDF plan approval or a minor works lodgement.

Sources: BCA, PSSCOC for Construction Works 2020, clauses 27 and 28, NTU insurance clauses under PSSCOC, 21 Feb 2022, Etiqa Contractors All Risks policy wording, SCDF plan approval for fire safety works
