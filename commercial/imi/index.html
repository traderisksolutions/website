The cover

Investment management insurance in Singapore

Investment management insurance combines professional indemnity, directors and officers liability and crime cover in one policy for fund managers, their funds and their directors. In Singapore the buyers are licensed fund management companies, venture capital fund managers, family offices and external asset managers. The trigger is the MAS licence: Retail LFMCs must hold minimum professional indemnity insurance scaled to assets under management, and institutional investors ask every manager for a certificate during operational due diligence.

It is not cover for investment losses caused by market movements, nor for a fund's own trading losses. Each section has its own trigger: PI is claims-made for breach of professional duty, D&O is claims-made for management wrongful acts, and crime is discovery-based for theft by staff or outsiders. The buyer sets a limit per section or one shared aggregate, a deductible, and an annual term; for a Retail LFMC the deductible is capped at 20% of base capital. The broker matches the wording to the MAS minimum and the fund documents.

Who buys it

  • Retail licensed fund management companies with a MAS minimum PII to evidence.
  • A/I licensed fund managers and VCFMs meeting institutional investor due diligence.
  • Single and multi-family offices managing third-party or related-party money.
  • External asset managers and robo-advisers holding client mandates.
  • General partners and fund directors who need D&O on the fund vehicles.
  • REIT and private credit managers with trustee-imposed cover requirements.

Price and terms

What moves the premium

Assets under management
AUM sets both the MAS minimum and the rating base; the Retail LFMC minimum rises from S$2 million below S$100 million AUM to S$25 million at S$10 billion.
Strategy
Private credit, digital assets and leveraged strategies are rated higher than long-only equity mandates.
Investor base
Retail and US investors raise claims exposure and the premium; institutional-only mandates rate lower.
Controls
An independent administrator and custodian, segregation of duties and dual payment authority reduce the crime rate.
Limit structure
One shared aggregate costs less than separate towers for PI, D&O and crime; separate towers protect the directors when PI is exhausted.
Regulatory history
MAS inspection findings, investor complaints and prior notifications are priced in or excluded.

At renewal

Three mistakes we see most often

Deductible above the MAS cap
A Retail LFMC accepts a large excess to cut premium and the PII no longer meets the guideline, which caps the deductible at 20% of base capital. Set the deductible against base capital first and price the premium second.
Legal costs eat the minimum
The policy limit equals the MAS minimum but defence costs sit inside it, so the amount left for client claims drops below the required figure. Buy costs in addition to the limit, or raise the limit to absorb them, as the guideline directs.
Fund vehicles left off the schedule
A new fund or SPV is launched after inception and is not an insured entity when a claim arrives. Use a wording with automatic cover for new funds and notify launches within the policy period.

Questions

Questions buyers ask

What professional indemnity does MAS require of fund managers?

MAS sets a minimum PII for Retail LFMCs by assets under management: S$2 million below S$100 million AUM, S$5 million from S$200 million, S$11 million from S$500 million, S$21 million from S$1 billion and S$25 million from S$10 billion. The minimum applies to each of three baseline areas: breach of professional duty, infidelity or dishonesty, and loss of documents of title. The appendix does not set a figure for A/I LFMCs or VCFMs.

Why combine PI, D&O and crime in one policy?

One investor complaint can trigger all three: a mandate breach is a PI claim, the director who approved it faces a D&O claim, and a payment diverted by a staff member is a crime loss. A combined policy removes the argument between three insurers over which section pays. The trade-off is one aggregate limit; a separate Side A tower for directors can be added.

Does it cover the funds as well as the manager?

It can. The insured entities should list the management company, each fund vehicle, the general partner and any SPV. Fund directors, including independent directors on Cayman or Singapore VCC boards, are insured persons. Wordings differ on automatic cover for new funds; some include funds launched during the year within a size threshold, others require endorsement.

What is the crime section for?

Direct financial loss of the manager's or the fund's money from employee dishonesty, forgery, computer fraud, funds transfer fraud and third-party theft. It is discovery-based: the loss must be discovered during the policy period. It does not cover trading losses, underperformance or losses the manager cannot prove. Social engineering fraud is often a sub-limited extension and should be checked.

Can a group policy from an overseas parent count?

MAS may accept a group PII if its cover is at least five times the standalone minimum, or a letter of undertaking from a parent of satisfactory financial standing with liability equal to or above the minimum. If the group deductible exceeds 20% of the Singapore entity's base capital, a parent undertaking for the excess is required. The broker documents this for the MAS file.

What base capital is the deductible measured against?

Base capital is S$1,000,000 for fund management of a collective investment scheme offered to retail investors, S$500,000 for non-CIS management for retail customers and S$250,000 for managers serving only accredited or institutional investors. An LFMC must also hold financial resources of at least 120% of its total risk requirement. The PII deductible for a Retail LFMC must not exceed 20% of base capital.

Sources: MAS: Guidelines on Licensing and Conduct of Business for FMCs (SFA 04-G05), 10 Dec 2024, MAS: SFA 04-G05 guideline page
