What trade credit insurance covers

Insolvency Protection

Immediate indemnification of outstanding invoices if a major commercial client enters corporate liquidation or bankruptcy.

Protracted Default Recovery

Financial backup when corporate buyers fail to settle valid, undisputed invoices within the agreed credit period.

Political Risk Extensions

Safeguards cross-border receivables against government moratoria, currency transfer restrictions, or unexpected import/export bans.

Credit Intelligence & Monitoring

Active credit risk assessments and real-time monitoring of your buyers' financial health to prevent bad debt exposure.
