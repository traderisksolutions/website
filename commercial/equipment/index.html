The cover

Machinery breakdown insurance in Singapore

Machinery breakdown insurance pays to repair or replace plant that fails suddenly from an internal cause: a short circuit, a casting defect, a bearing that seizes, a centrifugal tear, low water in a boiler, operator error. In Singapore it is bought by manufacturers, precision engineering shops, cold chain operators, printers, building owners with chillers and lifts, and clinics with imaging equipment. The trigger is usually a repair quotation that the property insurer has declined.

It is not property insurance: fire, lightning, flood, theft and collapse are excluded here and belong to the fire or all risks policy, which in turn excludes a machine's own mechanical or electrical breakdown. Cover is annual, per machine listed in a schedule, at new replacement value including freight, duties and erection, with a deductible per occurrence. The broker aligns the two policies so no cause falls between them and adds loss of profits or stock deterioration where the business needs it.

Who buys it

  • Manufacturers running CNC machines, presses, injection moulders and compressors.
  • Cold stores and food processors whose refrigeration plant protects perishable stock.
  • Building owners and MCSTs with chillers, lifts, generators and switchgear.
  • Printers, packaging plants and component makers with high-value single machines.
  • Clinics, imaging centres and laboratories with scanners and analysers out of warranty.
  • Data centres and offices with UPS systems, generators and cooling plant.

What the insurer needs to quote

  • Schedule of machines: make, model, year, serial number, new replacement value
  • Maintenance records and service contracts for the last 12 months
  • Operating hours and shifts per day
  • Location, foundations and any outdoor or marine exposure
  • Breakdown history for three years with repair costs
  • Extensions needed: loss of profits, stock deterioration, express freight

Price and terms

What moves the premium

Replacement value of each machine
Premium is a rate on the new replacement value, so the schedule total sets the base.
Machine type and age
Boilers, turbines, presses and older plant rate higher than modern packaged units; insurers may inspect first.
Maintenance and service contracts
Documented preventive maintenance and a manufacturer service contract reduce the rate; the policy requires compliance with manufacturers' recommendations.
Deductible
A deductible per occurrence, as a fixed sum or a percentage of the item value, lowers premium.
Extensions
Loss of profits, deterioration of stock in cold storage, express freight and overtime are each rated on top.
Claims record
Breakdowns in the last three years, insured or not, are declared and rated.

At renewal

Three mistakes we see most often

Insuring at book value
The wording requires the sum insured to equal the cost of a new machine of the same kind, including freight, duties and erection; a depreciated book value triggers proportional reduction of every claim. Ask the supplier for a current replacement quotation at each renewal.
Claiming after the repair
The policy pays nothing for a loss notified more than 14 days after it happened, and the insurer has the right to inspect before repairs start. Notify the broker the day the machine stops and keep the damaged parts.
Breakdown and property policies that do not meet
The property policy excludes own mechanical or electrical breakdown; the breakdown policy excludes fire, flood and theft. A motor that short-circuits and then burns leaves two insurers arguing over cause. Place both lines with one insurer, or agree in writing how a joint loss is split.

Questions

Questions buyers ask

What causes of breakdown are covered?

Unforeseen and sudden physical damage from causes such as defects in casting and material, faulty design, faults in erection, bad workmanship, lack of skill, carelessness, shortage of water in boilers, physical explosion, tearing apart by centrifugal force and short circuit, plus any other cause the wording does not exclude. Cover applies after performance acceptance tests, whether the machine is working, idle, being cleaned, overhauled or moved within the premises.

What is excluded?

Fire, lightning, chemical explosion, theft, flood, earthquake and collapse, which belong on the property policy. Also excluded: wear and tear, cavitation, erosion, corrosion and boiler scale; exchangeable tools and wear parts such as dies, moulds, belts, ropes, tyres and refractory linings; defects known at inception; loss a supplier, contractor or repairer is responsible for by law or contract; and consequential loss unless a loss of profits section is added.

How is a claim paid?

If the machine can be repaired, the insurer pays the repair cost, dismantling and re-erection, and ordinary freight, with no deduction for depreciation on replaced parts. If the repair cost reaches the machine's actual value before the breakdown, it is treated as destroyed and the insurer pays that actual value, which is replacement value less depreciation. Overtime and express freight are paid only if agreed in writing beforehand.

Does the policy cover a machine still under warranty?

Not for failures the manufacturer, supplier or repairer is responsible for by law or under contract; those are excluded. Breakdown from operator error, an electrical surge inside the premises or causes outside the warranty remains covered. Most buyers add a machine to the schedule when its warranty expires, or insure it from day one and declare the warranty terms.

Can I insure the production loss from a breakdown?

Yes, as a machinery loss of profits section or a separate policy, written on the same gross profit and indemnity period basis as fire consequential loss. It pays the reduction in gross profit and increased cost of working during the time the insured machine is out of action after a covered breakdown, less any savings. Insurers set a time excess of several days per occurrence.

Is deterioration of stock in cold rooms covered?

Only by extension. A deterioration of stock section pays for goods in cold storage that spoil after a covered breakdown of the refrigeration plant, or after a failure of the public electricity supply where that extension is also bought. Stock must be listed with its value and the plant must be on the breakdown schedule.
